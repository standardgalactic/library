# Licensing boundaries and preliminary third-party inventory

The Holographic Commons Dedication covers only rights held by Flyxion in
original contributions. It does not place every repository file in the public
domain. Source passages retain their own terms alongside original commentary.

This preliminary inventory reviews main at
`7749fb64913708b1380652f91ae2fdc13321de04`. It is not exhaustive clearance
of PDFs, images, audio, quotations, or other assets. Missing author metadata
does not establish ownership or public-domain status.

## Fonts

`fonts/dactyl.ttf`, `fonts/Systada.ttf`, and `fonts/Systada-Regular.ttf`
contain a 2019 notice naming Andrew O'Rourke and identifying SIL OFL 1.1.
`fonts/NovaMonoStandardGalactic.ttf` identifies wmk69, a 2011 copyright,
OFL 1.1, and the Reserved Font Name NovaMono. Their applicable OFL terms
remain in force. Related conversions and editable sources require provenance
checks as well. Conversion does not remove upstream rights.

The entire `fonts/` directory is excluded pending provenance review, including
fonts with incomplete metadata. This does not extend to ordinary documents
merely typeset with OFL fonts. The authoritative OFL text is available at
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/; this reference
does not replace required accompanying notices.

## Mixed-source archives

`free-lunch-vibes.txt` contains Cory Doctorow's Little Brother material and
an Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 notice.

`fruit-farming-megastructures.txt`, `pantomimetic-origin-of-language.txt`,
and `exploring-genetic-similarities.txt` contain article passages bearing
CC BY 4.0 notices. The second also includes a review attributed to B. Elan
Dresher requiring its own rights assessment.

`uncanny-valley-detector.txt` and `active-inference-glossary.txt` contain
material bearing a 2017 AAAI notice. The latter also includes material
attributed to Forrest Bishop bearing a 1994-2007 reserved-rights notice.

`mechanical-design-principles.txt` includes Project Gutenberg material and
notices. Public-domain status of a historical work does not automatically
apply to every translation, editorial addition, or accompanying notice.

These third-party portions are excluded from the dedication. Their presence
does not determine whether an existing use is lawful or unlawful. These are
examples, not a complete list of mixed-source files.

## Contributions and prior terms

Outside contributors' rights are not waived on their behalf. Contributions
previously supplied under Apache-2.0 retain applicable terms unless the rights
holder separately authorizes another release. The former license text is
preserved in `LICENSE-APACHE-2.0`.

Reviewed history includes commit
`8e4976139585c205a8cd7a5037922d6b6b760157` attributed to `utkarshcs18`.
GitHub returned an empty changed-file list; the identity alone does not
establish a surviving copyrightable contribution or permission to waive it.

Future contributors should expressly identify the license or dedication they
authorize and preserve third-party provenance and notices. Submission alone
does not adopt this dedication or waive a contributor's rights. Maintainers
should resolve licensing scope before merge.
