This is a much more interesting paper than a typical "we improved accuracy by 0.3%" paper because its primary goal is not higher performance. Instead, it asks:


Can we deliberately shape what a neural network learns so that its explanations correspond to concepts humans actually use?


The central idea is surprisingly simple.
